JARVIS GLASSES V4 QUICK START

1. Build/install V4.
2. Open it and connect M02S.
3. Press Test Wake Action Now.
4. iPhone Settings > Accessibility > Vocal Shortcuts.
5. Add Action > Wake Jarvis.
6. Train the phrase "Hey Jarvis" (or anything you want).
7. Leave Vocal Shortcuts ON.
8. Say the phrase with the app in the background.
9. Lock the phone and test again.

This System Wake method is the recommended one. The old app speech listener is only a backup.
